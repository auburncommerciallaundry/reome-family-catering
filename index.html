About Reome Family Catering

Brian Reome was raised in Auburn, California.  He grew up in a large family and he learned many cooking techniques from his grandmother, who was a local restaurant owner.  He has always enjoyed cooking over an open fire for his family and friends.  His passion for cooking only grew during his recent trips to Spain, where he was educated on different cooking styles and techniques.

We bring the fire to you..

Our specialties include cooking on an open fire for meals such as paella, skirt steak, hogs, lambs, and Basque chorizo, and a variety of tapas etc.  We also offer California favorites such as prime rib, chicken cordon bleu, and tuscan chicken.  All of these meals have excellent flavor and receive great reviews!
